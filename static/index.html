Daybook

A journal that
remembers

Write a few lines a day. Daybook remembers what you wrote, picks the thread back up tomorrow, and shows you what keeps returning.

  • Never a blank page. Every day opens with a question about something you wrote before.
  • Reflect on what you wrote. Daybook reads your entry and answers once, in its own space below it.
  • See what keeps coming back. Daybook reads weeks of your writing and tells you what you return to most, and how it is changing.

Your entries stay private to your account. Nobody else can read them.
